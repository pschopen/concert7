Concert7 - open source true stereo reverb

This installs the Concert7 VST3 plugin into C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3 and, optionally, the standalone app.

Impulse responses required
Concert7 does not include impulse responses. After installing, open Concert7 in your DAW: its setup screen
guides you through installing an impulse response library (it links to two free Bricasti M7 libraries
Concert7 is tested with; other libraries and your own impulse responses work too).

Concert7 is free software (GPLv3; the binaries are distributed under the AGPLv3 because they include JUCE).
Source code: https://github.com/pschopen/concert7

Concert7 is not affiliated with Bricasti or with the makers of the libraries.
